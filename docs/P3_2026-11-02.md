# Interview: P3

*Example interview. Fictional participant, created for teaching.*

- **Date:** 2026-11-02
- **Consent given:** Yes. Declined recording, so notes were taken by hand
- **Format:** In person at a coffee shop, 15 minutes
- **Who they are:** Member of a printmaking collective in Queens for 8 months. Student and part-time worker
- **Inside or outside my circle:** Outside. Referred by P1

## Background

**1. Tell me about the studio or space you are part of.**

"It's a collective with shared presses. Around 12 members. We pay monthly and each have keys."

**2. How long have you been a member, and what is your role?**

"About eight months. I'm a regular member. I mostly come in on weekends."

## Working together

**3. Tell me about the last event or project your studio organized together.**

"A zine fair table. We shared one table and everyone contributed work."

**4. Walk me through how the tasks were divided.**

"We talked about it at a meeting. Someone said they'd make a list of who's doing what. There's a list somewhere, but nobody checks it."

**5. How did members find out what still needed to be done?**

"I didn't, really. I saw the event on Instagram before anyone told me the details."

## Commitments

**6. Tell me about the last time a shared task did not get done. What happened?**

"Restocking ink. Everyone thought someone else was doing it. We ran out in the middle of a print session."

**7. Tell me about the last time you volunteered for something and could not follow through.**

"I said yes to helping at the zine fair because everyone was looking at me. Saying no in front of everyone feels awkward. Then I had a shift and couldn't go."

**Follow-up: Have you tried any tools to keep track?**

"We tried Trello once. Members said they stopped checking it after a week. It felt like extra work for something small."

## Closing

- **Anything else?** "If it was right in front of me, I'd do it. I just don't go looking for it."
- **Anyone else to speak with?** None
- **Follow-up later in the semester?** Yes

## Synthesis

- **Key quotes:**
  - "There's a list somewhere, but nobody checks it."
  - "Saying no in front of everyone feels awkward."
  - "If it was right in front of me, I'd do it. I just don't go looking for it."
- **What they actually do:** agrees to tasks in meetings, does not check any list afterward, finds out about events from social media
- **Surprise:** volunteering in a group can create pressure to say yes to tasks they cannot do
- **Workarounds:** the collective tried Trello. Members stopped using it after a week
