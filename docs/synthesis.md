# Synthesis

Members and organizers at three shared studios described the same pattern: commitments are made in person and then disappear. Tasks are agreed on in meetings, but tracked in group chats that most members mute. The work falls to whoever notices it first, usually an organizer. What surprised me: no one described the problem as people being unreliable. Members wanted to help, but could not see what still needed doing. The real problem is not a lack of commitment. It is that commitments become invisible after the meeting ends.

# Problem statement
Members of shared studios agree to tasks and events in person, but those commitments are tracked in places members do not check. As a result, the work falls to the few people who notice, and those people burn out.

# How might we
How might we keep shared commitments visible after a meeting ends?
