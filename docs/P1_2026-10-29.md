# Interview: P1

*Example interview. Fictional participant, created for teaching.*

- **Date:** 2026-10-29
- **Consent given:** Yes, recorded with permission
- **Format:** In person, 18 minutes
- **Who they are:** Member of a shared ceramics studio in Brooklyn for 2 years. Works full time. Uses the studio on weeknights and some weekends
- **Inside or outside my circle:** Outside. Met through a friend of a friend

## Background

**1. Tell me about the studio or space you are part of.**

"It's about 14 of us. We split rent, and everyone has a shelf and time on the wheels. There's no manager. We run it ourselves, which is great until something needs to get done."

**2. How long have you been a member, and what is your role?**

"Two years. I don't have an official role. I help with cleaning and sometimes the glaze orders."

## Working together

**3. Tell me about the last event or project your studio organized together.**

"We did a holiday sale in December. Everyone sold their work. It went well, but the week before was chaos. Nobody knew who was bringing tables."

**4. Walk me through how the tasks were divided.**

"At the monthly meeting, someone read a list of tasks and people raised their hands. Someone wrote it down on their phone. I don't know who. I never saw the list again."

**5. How did members find out what still needed to be done?**

"Mostly by showing up and seeing it wasn't done. Or someone would post in the group chat, 'Who was getting the tablecloths?' and then a lot of people would say 'not me.'"

## Commitments

**6. Tell me about the last time a shared task did not get done. What happened?**

"Deep cleaning. We said we'd clean on Sunday and two people showed up. Everyone else forgot or had something come up. We cleaned for four hours."

**7. Tell me about the last time you volunteered for something and could not follow through.**

"I said I'd help with the open studio and then work got busy. I felt bad, but I didn't know who to tell. So I didn't tell anyone. I just didn't go."

**Follow-up: How did you find out about changes to plans?**

"Sometimes I don't. I didn't know the meeting moved until I got there and the studio was empty."

## Closing

- **Anything else?** "I think people want to help. It's just easy to forget when nothing reminds you."
- **Anyone else to speak with?** Suggested a member of a printmaking collective in Queens
- **Follow-up later in the semester?** Yes

## Synthesis
