# Interview: P2

*Example interview. Fictional participant, created for teaching.*

- **Date:** 2026-10-31
- **Consent given:** Yes, recorded with permission
- **Format:** Video call, 22 minutes
- **Who they are:** Co-founder and organizer of a shared art and design studio in Bushwick. Organizes the monthly open studio event
- **Inside or outside my circle:** Outside. Contacted through the studio's Instagram

## Background

**1. Tell me about the studio or space you are part of.**

"We have about 20 members. Painters, a few designers, a woodworker. We host an open studio once a month where the public can come in and see work."

**2. How long have you been a member, and what is your role?**

"Four years. I'm one of the founders, so I end up being the organizer for most things, even though we're supposed to share it."

## Working together

**3. Tell me about the last event or project your studio organized together.**

"Last month's open studio. About 80 people came. It was a good night."

**4. Walk me through how the tasks were divided.**

"At the monthly meeting I listed what we needed: flyers, drinks, setup, someone at the door. People volunteered. In the meeting everyone volunteers. By Thursday it's quiet."

**5. How did members find out what still needed to be done?**

"I post reminders in the group chat. But the group chat has 200 messages a day. I just mute it, and I know everyone else does too. So the reminders get buried."

## Commitments

**6. Tell me about the last time a shared task did not get done. What happened?**

"The week before the open studio, I had no idea what was done. Did someone order drinks? Were flyers posted? I had to message people one by one. Half didn't answer. On the day, I did the rest myself."

**7. Tell me about the last time you volunteered for something and could not follow through.**

"Honestly, it's usually the other way. I end up doing it because I'm the one who notices. If I don't check, nobody checks."

**Follow-up: How does that feel?**

"Tiring. The event went well, but I told my partner I don't want to organize the next one. I love the space. I just can't keep being the reminder."

## Closing

- **Anything else?** "It's not that people are flaky. They just can't see what's left to do."
- **Anyone else to speak with?** Suggested two members of their studio and an organizer at a coworking space
- **Follow-up later in the semester?** Yes

## Synthesis

- **Key quotes:**
  - "In the meeting everyone volunteers. By Thursday it's quiet."
  - "The group chat has 200 messages a day. I just mute it."
  - "I end up doing it because I'm the one who notices."
- **What they actually do:** lists tasks at meetings, posts reminders in the group chat, then messages people one by one the week before
- **Surprise:** the organizer does not blame members. The problem is that no one can see what is left to do
- **Workarounds:** individual messages, doing the remaining tasks alone
